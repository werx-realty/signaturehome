Our Process

Four steps to welcome home

Communication, attention to detail and a commitment to turning your vision into reality are at the heart of how we build. We guide you through each step for a positive, seamless experience.

01

Perfect Homesite

Already have a lot you love? We can move straight to building. Still looking? We'll help you find the right location.

02

Design your Home

Work with a plan designer or architect on a custom design that fits your style and needs — we help plan the interior and exterior.

03

Build your Home

As your builder and contractor, we manage construction start to finish, keep you updated on milestones and choose finishes and fixtures together.

04

Love your Home

We hand you the keys to your newly built custom home. Welcome home!
